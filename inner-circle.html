Why Zorya exists

Closing the gender gap in AI through education and innovation

Zorya AI is an entrepreneurial network for women already running companies: technologically skilled, AI-native, building the next generation of businesses, transforming their business models from the inside.

The technology defining the next decade is being designed, funded and deployed in rooms where women are still a minority, and that gap doesn't close with awareness campaigns. It closes when the women already running companies have the fluency to build with AI themselves, and the standing to argue about it.

So we built the room we couldn't find: small enough that everyone speaks, senior enough that nobody has to explain themselves, practical enough that you leave with a system running in your business, not notes about one.

Both of us are Slovenian. We've built our lives in other European countries, Maja in Spain and Sara in Germany, but we're building this one at home. Women hold 22% of AI roles globally, 15% at executive level, and Slovenia doesn't have a single AI community built for women. We're building the first one.

The work itself stays international. Cohorts are cross-border by design, open to founders from anywhere: the questions just happen to come from markets that regulate, fund and adopt AI at very different speeds.

We choose who joins, deliberately: values fit matters as much as readiness. A seat here isn't something you can buy.

Zorya AI sits alongside our other brands, SheAI, Innervoices, Builders and Lingocare, which fund and feed it. Same mission across all four: put the tools in the hands of people who were handed a chatbot instead of a seat at the table.
