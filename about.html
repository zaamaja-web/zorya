Why Zorya exists

Closing the gender gap in AI through education and innovation

Zorya AI is an ecosystem, an entrepreneurial network of female entrepreneurs: technologically skilled, AI-native in both transformation and implementation, building the new generation of companies. Dedicated to the education, advancement and innovation of women-led companies through the transformation of their business models.

The technology defining the next decade is being designed, funded and deployed in rooms where women are still a minority. That gap does not close with awareness campaigns. It closes when the women already running companies have the fluency to build with AI themselves, and the standing to argue about it.

So we built the room we could not find: small enough that everyone speaks, senior enough that nobody has to explain themselves, and practical enough that you leave with a system running in your business rather than notes about one.

Both of us are Slovenian, and neither of us works from Slovenia: Maja from Spain, Sara from Germany. That has made Zorya AI intra-European by default, the cohorts are cross-border, and the questions come from markets that regulate, fund and adopt AI at very different speeds.

We choose who we work with, deliberately. Values fit is part of selection alongside readiness, which is why a seat is not something you can simply buy.

Zorya AI sits alongside our other brands, SheAI, Innervoices, Builders and Lingocare, which fund and feed it. The mission is the same across all of them: put the tools in the hands of the people who were handed a chatbot instead of a seat at the table.
