The problem

AI without a system is just another tool

  • Teams try AI tools, but nothing connects and nothing runs without the person who set it up.
  • Staff use AI anyway, without a policy, on organizational data.
  • Since February 2025, the EU AI Act (Article 4) asks organizations to ensure staff who use AI have enough AI literacy.
