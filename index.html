The Inner Circle

An ecosystem, not a course platform

Most AI education is written for engineers or for beginners. The Inner Circle sits where our members actually work: making decisions about tools, teams and money with incomplete information. Education first, then advancement, then innovation. Inside it runs AI-First Leaders, the 10-week accelerator where the building happens.

Questions

Who is Zorya AI for? +

Founders, CEOs and solopreneurs already running something who want AI to make it better. No technical background needed, a willingness to build during the session is.

How do you choose who gets a seat? +

Selection is a conversation, not a payment. We look for shared ethics and a business we are glad to help scale, alongside readiness to build.

How much time does it take? +

One live workshop a month, ninety minutes, plus an open studio hour. Everything is recorded, and most members spend another hour or two applying it.

Is it only for women? +

The Inner Circle is built for and by women leaders. Corporate training engagements are open to whole teams.

What does it cost? +

The 90-minute AI Leadership event is free, and it is where everyone starts. From there, a small number of leaders are invited into AI-First Leaders, the 10-week accelerator: €6,000, with early bird places at 30% off (about €4,200). Ten seats per cohort.

Is there a limit on places? +

Ten founders, ten weeks, ten seats per cohort, by invitation. We select on values fit as well as readiness, so every session stays a conversation and everyone in the room is building something real.
