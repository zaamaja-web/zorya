Why Zorya exists

Closing the gender gap in AI through education and innovation

Zorya AI is an entrepreneurial network for women already running companies: technologically skilled, AI-native, building the next generation of businesses, transforming their business models from the inside.

The technology defining the next decade is being designed, funded and deployed in rooms where women are still a minority, and that gap doesn't close with awareness campaigns. It closes when the women already running companies have the fluency to build with AI themselves, and the standing to argue about it.

So we built the room we couldn't find: small enough that everyone speaks, senior enough that nobody has to explain themselves, practical enough that you leave with a system running in your business, not notes about one.

Both of us are Slovenian. We've built our lives in other European countries, Maja in Spain and Sara in Germany, but we're building this one at home. Women hold 22% of AI roles globally, 15% at executive level, and Slovenia doesn't have a single AI community built for women. We're building the first one.

The work itself stays international. Cohorts are cross-border by design, open to founders from anywhere: the questions just happen to come from markets that regulate, fund and adopt AI at very different speeds.

We choose who joins, deliberately: values fit matters as much as readiness. A seat here isn't something you can buy.

Zorya AI sits alongside our other brands, SheAI, Innervoices, Builders and Lingocare, which fund and feed it. Same mission across all four: put the tools in the hands of people who were handed a chatbot instead of a seat at the table.

Mission Europe

Our commitment to the women shaping Europe's AI.


This is our manifesto, the place we point to when someone asks what Zorya AI is for. It paints a picture of why we exist, how we work, where we are going, and the accountability we hold ourselves to.

We wrote it for the ambitious women who build, create value, and lead mindfully, for the partners and funders who back this work, for the people who decide how Europe governs AI, and for everyone working to make Europe stronger in it.

Where Europe stands

Europe has set itself a bold ambition. It intends to become a leading AI continent, to move from writing the world's most comprehensive rules to putting the technology to work across its industries, and to grow its digital workforce from 9 million to 20 million professionals by the end of this decade.

The foundations are here. Compute capacity is being built across the Union, with regional access points for everyone who works with borrowed infrastructure.

Europe's gender equality strategy treats artificial intelligence as a field of its own for the first time. An action plan aims to make Europe the most attractive place in the world for women in research, innovation, and enterprise by 2030.

Yet today, women hold roughly one in five AI roles worldwide and about one in six of the senior ones. Europe finds its eleven million additional professionals by opening the path for the women who are already here.

We believe that there's abundant talent in Europe; what it needs are systems that carry that talent upward, and a way of working as one continent on a question that twenty seven countries are each answering alone.

20 million digital professionals Europe aims for by 2030, up from nine million today.
22% AI roles worldwide are held by women, and 13% of the senior ones.
27 countries currently answer the same question separately.

What we are

Zorya AI is the place for women shaping Europe's AI. For founders, for women in leadership, for researchers, and for those deciding how AI is put to work.

Our rooms stay open across that whole range: across countries large and small, across sectors and generations, and across the many different paths that lead a woman to the point where she decides about technology.

It is about belonging among women who find someone at eye level here. And it is about craft: programmes, support through the actual build, honest exchange, and a home base in every country we enter.

We built the room we searched for. Small enough that everyone speaks. Experienced enough that every woman arrives already understood. Practical enough that each one leaves with something running.

The room is for women. The work it does is for Europe.

How we work

We start with the women who are already in motion, and we do so deliberately.

01

Strengthen

Women who lead today are Europe's early adopters. They bring the conviction, and we add the capacity to execute. Their decisions take effect immediately, in real companies, real budgets and real teams. We enable them first because they are the first who can pass it on.

02

Multiply

Each of them carries this into her team, her company and her country, and that transmission is what reaches scale. In every country we build our own team that leads the work on the ground, in the local language and in the local setting. We open our programmes, formats and tools to the independent women's, technology and founder initiatives across Europe, so that each of them becomes stronger at home.

03

Anchor

From the network comes infrastructure, which is the thing that lasts: shared programmes, open resources, reliable data, and a voice where decisions are made. Infrastructure is what reaches the whole of Europe, and reaches it for a long time.

From Ljubljana outward

We begin in Slovenia. The next two countries are Germany and Spain. In every country we build our own team with a home base, led by women from that setting, and alongside it a partner initiative that already works there. By the end of 2030 we intend to have a Zorya AI team in every European country, and the order of the steps after Germany and Spain takes shape together with our partners on the ground.

  • SloveniaWhere it begins, in 2026.
  • Germany and SpainThe next two countries with their own team.
  • All of EuropeA Zorya AI team in every European country by the end of 2030.

What we build toward

That AI capability reaches the women who are still outside it today, whatever their background, education or means. And that it comes to sit inside Europe's education and training systems.

European law now expects the people who deploy AI to understand what they deploy. That turns capability into an obligation, and it makes this the moment when serious training reaches women.

We work in the languages of the places we enter, because a European network earns that name by speaking many. And we intend that talent beyond Europe finds a way here, through circular mobility and partnerships that build capacity in both directions.

The same capability serves as protection. Systems trained on the past repeat it, and women meet that reality in hiring, in credit, in health, and in the rising volume of synthetic images and online abuse aimed at them. Women who understand these systems can name what they see, challenge it with evidence, and build differently. We treat that as part of the craft.

How we stand on our own

We carry ourselves through what we deliver. Membership and programmes fund this place. That is the precondition for our position, because a house that stands on its own stays free to speak.

We are open about the sequence this creates: those who invest in programmes make possible the open resources, partnerships and access that reach everyone else. We prefer to state that plainly.

An independent voice

Europe has strong initiatives for women in technology. What comes next is a shared, independent institution that speaks for the women who decide about AI.

We are forming an independent advisory board and a strategic council. Both will be appointed against published criteria, for a fixed term, and answerable to their own mandate alone. They bring together women from different countries, sectors and generations: founders, researchers, lawyers, policy makers and leaders from industry. Their task is to advise national and European policy, to publish what they find, and to make the case with evidence.

We will build them at the moment we can build them in full independence, and we will take the time that requires, because an institution that arrives free serves everyone, and serves for a long time.

Until then we earn that right. We take part in the European processes already underway, we support the commitments Europe has made to women in its digital economy, and we bring what we learn in our own rooms to the people writing the next set of rules.

The rules we hold ourselves to

What separates us from a consultancy is the set of rules we live by.

  • The advisory board and the strategic council govern themselves.
  • We publish each year where our funding comes from.
  • The independent voice draws its funding from sources outside the industry it assesses.
  • A seat here is earned.
  • We disclose any interest we hold in what we recommend.
  • The data of the women who trust us stays theirs, under European standards, in every country where we work.
